Featured Project

Artifact 01 · AI Lab

Smart Business Analytics Assistant

Audience: Recruiters, hiring managers, and professionals interested in AI and Business Analytics.

Introduction & Objective: I developed an AI assistant prototype designed to help small business owners understand sales performance, calculate basic KPIs, interpret Excel formulas, and consider affordable improvement strategies.

Process: I followed the Design Thinking stages—Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test—to identify user needs, design the assistant, and evaluate its responses.

Tools & Technologies: ChatGPT, Plugin Creator, generative AI prompting, KPI calculations, and Excel formula guidance.

Testing: I evaluated three hypothetical scenarios: a coffee shop, a clothing store, and a beauty salon. All three met my selected evaluation criteria. The assistant calculated numerical changes when data were provided and avoided inventing causes when information was missing.

Value Proposition & Unique Value: This prototype translates business analytics concepts into clear, accessible guidance for owners who may not have a dedicated analyst or a large marketing budget.

Relevance: The project demonstrates problem framing, AI prototyping, business metric interpretation, and responsible evaluation—skills relevant to entry-level analytics and AI roles.
