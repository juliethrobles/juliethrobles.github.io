ARTIFACT 04 · ENERGY | OIL & GAS

AI Data Readiness for Predictive Maintenance in Oil & Gas

Julieth Robles · Applied AI case study · November 2026

Business problem

A hypothetical oil and gas company wants to anticipate pump and compressor failures. Sensor readings and maintenance records may be incomplete, duplicated or inconsistent. The objective is to establish data readiness before training a model.

Data quality framework

Preserve raw data; profile temperature, pressure, vibration and maintenance records; investigate missing readings; standardize units and timestamps; validate failure labels; document lineage; split data chronologically and prevent related records or post-failure information from leaking into evaluation.

Privacy and governance

Consider federated learning when facilities cannot centralize raw records. Harmonize data definitions, use secure aggregation and assess model-update leakage. Assign data owners and document access rules. Federated learning does not guarantee privacy.

Validation and monitoring

Proposed indicators: completeness = valid readings / expected readings; duplicate rate = duplicate records / total records; label accuracy = correct audited labels / reviewed labels; traceability = records with documented origin / total records. Express each as a percentage. No measured values are available. Evaluate future models on unseen time periods and facilities, monitor missed failures and false alarms, and retain human oversight.

Academic connection and limitations

Adapted from Data Challenge Scenarios in Machine Learning Fundamentals. The project translates data cleaning, distribution shift and privacy concepts into an energy industry use case. This AI-assisted conceptual study uses no real industrial data or trained model and claims no operational savings.
