From industrial data to reliable decisions

Completeness

—

Valid readings / expected readings × 100%

Duplicate rate

—

Duplicate records / total records × 100%

Label accuracy

—

Correct audited labels / reviewed labels × 100%

Traceability

—

Records with known origin / total records × 100%

Proposed metrics only; no measured values. Future evaluation should monitor missed failures and false alarms on unseen time periods and facilities, retaining human oversight. Federated learning does not guarantee privacy.

05 / ACADEMIC CONNECTION

Scope and limitations

Adapted from Data Challenge Scenarios in Machine Learning Fundamentals. This AI-assisted conceptual study applies data cleaning, distribution shift and privacy concepts to an energy industry use case. No real industrial data, trained model or measured operational savings are presented.
